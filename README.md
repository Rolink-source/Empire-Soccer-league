# Empire-Soccer-league
Empire Soccer League is a 20-club soccer league with its own kits, rules and community. Browse every team and its colors, check the standings, schedule and top scorers, watch match videos, and see the Hall of Fame. Join the Discord to connect with players and stay up to date.
